# Character & Location Bible — बारह बजकर सत्रह मिनट

Source of truth for story facts: `source-text/Barah_Bajkar_Satrah_Minute_Hindi.txt`.
The manuscript gives almost no physical description. Everything marked **(VI)** is a
visual interpretation chosen for this film, recorded here so it stays consistent.
Rig definitions live in `src/rigs/characters.ts`; this document mirrors them.

| Code | When | Grade |

## Characters

### Meera Saxena, adult (~32) — `meeraAdult`
- Restores old audio recordings in Delhi. Daughter of Dinesh.
- **Silhouette (VI):** slim and upright, with a slight forward hunch when listening. Chin-length dark hair tucked behind one ear, with one strand that falls forward.
- **Face (VI):** long face, strong dark brows, deep-set eyes with tired under-eye shading, small silver studs.
- **Wardrobe (VI):**
  - Deep indigo kurta, charcoal trousers, grey-brown dupatta, thin black watch.
  - Outdoors in rain: an olive-grey raincoat (बरसाती, in the text).
  - Headphones around her neck in work scenes.
  - Memorial: an off-white kurta and the same dupatta.
- **Signature:** the strand of hair, and her hands. She is a sound editor, so hands are acted precisely.

### Meera, child (10) — `meeraChild`
- **(VI)** Two plaits with red ribbons, a faded blue school frock, the same strong brows as the adult.
- Appears in the hospital memory, the whistle afternoon, the flood fragments and the family-tape memory.

### Dinesh Saxena, old (recent past, as heard on tape and remembered) — `dineshOld`
- He died 11 days before the story begins. He is never shown "returning"; he appears only in the letter and diary inserts and as a framed photograph.
- **(VI)** Grey hair, thin and receding; rectangular spectacles; a grey moustache; a cardigan over a cream shirt.

### Dinesh Saxena, young (22 years earlier) — `dineshYoung`
- Responsible for the town siren. Switched it off for 8 minutes; later carried Meera out.
- **(VI)** Black side-parted hair, black moustache, rectangular spectacles (the continuity link to the old version), half-sleeve pale-blue shirt, dark trousers, a wristwatch.

### Mother (unnamed in text) — `mother`
- Seen only in memories, often partly framed or in soft focus. Her full face is shown only in warm memories (the whistle afternoon and the family tape).
- **(VI)** Hair in a low bun, a red bindi, glass bangles, a maroon cotton sari with an ochre border. On the flood night the sari is wet and dark, and she is seen from behind or in silhouette.

### Anshu (younger brother, ~6) — `anshu`
- Died in the flood. Always seen in memory.
- **(VI)** Round face, a cowlick, a mustard half-sleeve shirt, brown shorts, and a small tin whistle on a red string. The whistle is his signature prop.

### Kabir (~33) — `kabir`
- Repairs electronics in the bazaar shop his father once ran (radios then, phone covers now). His father died rescuing others that night.
- **(VI)** Broad-shouldered, with a short beard and short wavy hair. Grey-green shirt with rolled sleeves, a canvas tool apron in the shop, a pencil behind his ear.
- A quiet presence: he stops tapes instantly and pulls up chairs.

### Vikram Vashishth (~55 in present) — `vikram`
- He ran the factory that night. Now a public figure with posters around town.
- **(VI)** Silver hair swept back, clean-shaven, heavy-lidded eyes, a crisp white kurta and a dark Nehru jacket, a gold ring.
- **In the tape-memory scene (flood night)** he is a **faceless silhouette** with an umbrella and a raincoat collar. His identity is revealed only by the narration in S09 and S17.

### Harish Kaka (elderly watchman) — `harish`
- **(VI)** Very thin, with a stooped back, white stubble, a woollen cap and an old khaki watchman's coat.
- **Signature:** a bunch of keys on an iron ring, which he carries in his pocket "since that night".

### Minor
- **Crowd at the memorial:** simplified townspeople with varied silhouettes. A woman in a green sari stands up («मेरी बहन उस फाटक पर थी»), and a man in a checked shirt asks the question.
- **Roadside father and daughter (S18):** strangers, drawn without detail.

## Locations (geography fixed once and reused)
- **Family house** (Sahajanpur, an old single-storey house):
  - **Front verandah:** three steps down to a lane; the **height mark** is in the right-hand corner pillar.
  - **Study** (back room): a window on the left (rain), the **wall clock** on the right wall above the desk, the desk below the clock, and the **tape almirah** on the far left wall beside the window.
  - **Outer room:** the door leads from the study into the outer room, where Kabir works.
- **Kabir's shop:** in the bazaar, with a glass front, phone covers hanging and a counter. The door is on the right.
- **Factory office (announcement room):**
  - Back wall: a desk with a ring mark and a separate siren switch box on the right.
  - Left wall: a window with a broken pane. Through it, left to right: the back verandah, then the dirt road and the **school gate**, only a few steps away.
- **School shelter gate:** a rusted iron double gate with a chain and padlock. Behind it are two rooms and a verandah; a dirt road runs outside.
- **Vikram's office:** modern and white, with a large window, a desk, and the **market model** on a side table. A glass door stands where the gate used to be.
- **Memorial courtyard:** the yard of the same factory office. A stage with marigolds, a stone with 17 names (Anshu's at the bottom), the microphone box, and plastic chairs.
- **Hospital verandah (memory):** a long corridor with pillars, benches, and white-sheeted shapes kept out of focus.

## Props
- The clock is always stopped at **12:17**: minute hand 102°, hour hand 8.5°, second hand fixed at 0°. It moves only from the battery moment (S22).
- **Cassette:** brown shell with a cream label in Dinesh's handwriting.
- **Recorder:** an old black portable, with the reels visible through a window. The reels turn only while it plays.
- **Kabir's second deck:** silver.
- **Laptop:** dark grey, showing a waveform UI.
- **Headphones:** black over-ear.
- **Diary:** a brown cloth-bound book holding the school ID card.
- **Letter:** lined paper with shaky handwriting.
- **Keys:** an iron ring with 5 keys.
- **Gate:** chain and padlock.
- **Anshu's whistle:** a tin whistle on a red string.
- **Height mark:** pencil lines and the name «अंशु».
- **Phone:** Meera's black smartphone.
